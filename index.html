Hi, I'm Karina Bittencourt a Broomfield Portrait Photographer. I deeply believe in photography that goes beyond simple documentation, always aiming to capture the essence of the moment in an authentic and timeless way. For me, every shot must be intentional, balancing the beauty of aesthetics with a genuine narrative. My approach blends the fluidity of documentary photography with the sophistication of fine art and the dynamism of fashion photography. With a thoughtful eye, no rush, and no impositions, this fusion of styles is what inspires and drives me to create images that don’t just document but truly speak to the heart of those who see them, creating memories that last forever.

Karina Bittencourt of KB Photographer is a portrait photography expert serving Broomfield, Summit County, and beyond in Colorado. Specializing in lifestyle, action, and adventure sessions, she captures unforgettable moments for couples, families, and micro weddings. Whether you're planning a vacation in the mountains, hitting the slopes for skiing or snowboarding, or simply enjoying the beauty of a resort, Karina blends the essence of sports, emotion, and story to create images that feel real, raw, and timeless.
