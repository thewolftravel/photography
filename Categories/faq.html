What types of sessions do you offer?

I specialize in lifestyle and branding photography, capturing everything from adventure-filled moments in the mountains to intimate family and couples sessions. Whether it’s for your personal memories, a professional branding shoot, or a snowboarding session, each photoshoot is tailored to reflect your story and vision.

How do I book a session?

Booking a session is easy! Simply reach out via my contact page or email me directly at hello@kbphotographer.com with your vision, location, and preferred date. I’ll get back to you with availability, package options, and next steps. Let’s make something amazing together!

Where do your photoshoots take place?

I work in the beautiful mountain regions of Colorado, including Broomfield, Summit County, and various ski resorts. From stunning landscapes to cozy, intimate indoor settings, I’m always open to new locations that align with your vision. If you’re looking for something specific, let’s chat!

What should I wear for my photoshoot?

Your photos should reflect your authentic self! Choose outfits that make you feel confident and comfortable. For outdoor or snowboarding shoots, think about layers, textures, and colors that complement the natural environment. If you’re unsure, I can offer personalized wardrobe suggestions based on the session style and location.

Do you offer prints and albums?

Absolutely! I offer a full shop experience (try it here) with options for prints, albums, wall displays, digital downloads, greeting cards, and more. Every piece is created with the utmost care and quality. After your photoshoot, we can discuss the perfect way to showcase your memories.

How much do your sessions cost?

My pricing varies depending on the type of session, location, deliverables, and duration. For lifestyle sessions, it starts at $270 per hour. Wedding photography is priced at $250 per hour, with an additional $100/hour for a second photographer. For other sessions visit my pricing page here Investment. Payment plans are available! I also provide a customized quote based on your needs.

Do you travel outside of Colorado?

While I’m based in Colorado, I love to travel! If you’re looking for a photoshoot outside the state, just let me know here contact, and we can discuss travel options and pricing.

What is the turnaround time for my photos?

Typically, I provide a sneak peek of your photos within a week after the session. Full galleries are usually delivered within 2-4 weeks, depending on the season and session type.

Can I share my photos on social media?

Yes, of course! Once you receive your images, feel free to share them on social media, but please tag me ( @bittencourt_k) so we can spread the love. I also appreciate when clients mention my work when posting.

What if the weather is bad on the day of our shoot?

I’m all about embracing the moment, and a little weather can add character to your photos! Check out this Pinterest board. However, if conditions are unsafe or too extreme, I’m happy to reschedule your shoot. We’ll find a new date that works for you.

What time of day is best for a photoshoot?

The best time for a photoshoot is during early mornings or the golden hour—right before sunset. This is when the natural light is soft and warm, creating the perfect atmosphere for your photos. I work with natural light to capture genuine moments, and these times help bring out the best in both the surroundings and you!
