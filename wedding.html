FAQ

Do you offer engagement sessions?

Yes, I offer engagement sessions which are a great way to get comfortable in front of the camera and create beautiful images for your save-the-date cards and invitations.

What is included in your wedding photography package?

My packages include a pre-wedding consultation, full-day coverage, second photographer, high-resolution edited images, and a private online gallery for easy sharing with friends and family.

How far in advance should we book you?

I recommend booking as soon as you have your date to ensure availability, ideally 6-12 months in advance.

Can you recommend locations for our wedding portraits?

Absolutely, I have a list of beautiful locations and can help you choose the best spots that fit your vision.

How soon will we receive our photos after the wedding?

You can expect to receive your edited photos within 4-6 weeks after your wedding day.

We don't live in Colorado, how can we meet with you?

About half of my clients live elsewhere and hire me without an in-person meeting. I’m quick with emails and phone calls and Zoom is always helpful.
