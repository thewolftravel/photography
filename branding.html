FAQ

How should I prepare for my session?

Just bring your best self! We'll discuss your goals beforehand so you feel confident and ready. I'll guide you through poses and expressions to ensure we capture your best angles. Plus, I offer a style guide to help you choose the perfect outfit.

What are your pricing details?

I offer competitive rates tailored to your needs. Each package includes a consultation, the session, and a set of edited images. Contact me for a detailed quote!

What can I expect during the session?

Expect a relaxed, fun atmosphere. I'll guide you through the process, ensuring we get a variety of shots that showcase your unique personality and brand.

Do you offer makeup artist recommendations?

Absolutely! I have amazing makeup artists I can recommend to help you look your best for the session.

How long does a session typically last?

Sessions usually last between one to two hours, depending on the package and your specific needs. We'll ensure we have enough time to capture a variety of looks.

What kind of images will we capture?

Whether it's bio photos, working shots, or content for sales pages and social media, we'll create a tailored collection that captures your brand's essence. Every session is unique and personalized to ensure your photos stand out and effectively communicate your message.

Can I choose the location for my session?

Yes! We can shoot either indoors or outdoors, depending on your preference. If you have a specific location in mind, we can plan the session around that.

When will I receive my photos?

You can expect to receive your edited photos within two to three weeks of the session. I'll provide a selection of high-quality images ready for use on your website, social media, and marketing materials.
